Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 17, 2026 • Compliant with Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA 2023)

1. Introduction

ReviewBoost ("we," "our," or "us") provides a software-as-a-service platform that enables retail businesses to generate NFC and QR-assisted customer review portals and drafts. We are committed to protecting the personal data of our merchant subscribers and their customers.

2. Information We Collect

For Business Owners: When you register an account, we collect your business name, category, business address, Google Business Profile Place ID, phone number, and owner email address. For paid subscriptions, payment processing is performed securely via Razorpay; we do not store full payment card numbers or UPI PINs on our servers.

For Customers Scanning QR/NFC Cards: When customers rate a visit and request AI review drafts, we process selected tags and optional descriptive keywords to construct review text. For negative feedback submitted privately, we transmit the feedback message, rating, and optional contact details directly to the merchant management.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide, maintain, and personalize the review draft generation service.
  • To process subscription payments and recurring UPI Autopay billing via Razorpay.
  • To provide merchants with anonymized scan analytics and customer sentiment trends.
  • To protect our platform against abusive automated bots and security threats.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We share minimal necessary data with trusted third-party cloud infrastructure providers:

  • Razorpay: Secure payment processing and UPI Autopay mandate tokenization.
  • OpenAI / Google Gemini: AI natural language processing for review draft generation (customer notes are sanitized and capped).
  • Supabase: Encrypted cloud database and data storage.

5. Data Retention & Security

All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3/HTTPS. Passwords are salted and hashed using Argon2id. We retain business data for the duration of the merchant subscription. Merchants may request complete account deletion by contacting support.
