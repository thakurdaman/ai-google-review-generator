Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: October 17, 2026 • Razorpay Merchant Compliance

1. 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We want you to be fully confident in your investment. If you are a new merchant subscribing to ReviewBoost and find that the platform does not meet your expectations, you are entitled to a 100% full refund within 7 days of your initial payment date. No questions asked.

2. Subscription Cancellation

You may cancel your monthly or annual subscription at any time directly through your Merchant Dashboard $\rightarrow$ Billing section. Once cancelled:

  • Any future recurring UPI Autopay mandate debits will be stopped immediately.
  • Your customer portal and QR codes remain fully functional until the end of your current paid billing period.
  • No renewal charges will ever be levied after cancellation.

3. How to Request a Refund

To claim your 7-day refund, simply reach out to us with your registered business email and Razorpay Payment ID:

  • Email: support@reviewboost.local
  • WhatsApp / Phone: +91 98765 43210
  • Subject: "Refund Request - [Your Business Name]"

4. Refund Processing Timeline

Once approved, your refund will be initiated via the Razorpay payment gateway back to your original source of payment (UPI bank account, debit card, or net banking). Standard bank processing takes 5 to 7 business days to reflect in your account.
